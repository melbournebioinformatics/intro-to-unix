---
title: Learner Profiles
---

This workshop is designed for participants with little or no command-line experience.

**Priya** is a wet-lab biologist who has just generated her first sequencing dataset. Her analysis
tools only run from the command line and she has never opened a terminal. She wants enough Unix to
navigate the file system, inspect her files, and follow the command-line steps in other Melbourne
Bioinformatics workshops without feeling lost.

**Sam** is a postgraduate student who will soon need to run analyses on an HPC system such as
Spartan. He has copied and pasted commands before but doesn't understand paths, permissions, or how
to combine commands. He wants to build a confident mental model so he can work on a remote machine
and transfer files safely.
