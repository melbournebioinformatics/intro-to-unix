A hands-on introduction to the Unix command line for bioinformatics. This workshop is designed for
participants with little or no command-line knowledge, and provides the background skills required
for several other Melbourne Bioinformatics workshops that work on the command line.

This lesson is built with the [Melbourne Bioinformatics fork](https://github.com/melbournebioinformatics/workbench-template-md) of the [Carpentries Workbench](workbench). It was originally written by Steven Morgan for Melbourne Bioinformatics and adapted to The Carpentries Workbench.

For more information on Melbourne Bioinformatics, please see our [official page](mb-uom-page) and our [tutorial collection](mb-tutorials). If you would like to attend one of our workshops, please follow our [Eventbrite page](mb-eventbrite), where you can get notifications and register for upcoming workshops.
